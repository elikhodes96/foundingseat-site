For founders before their first sales hire

Prove the role, then fill it.

Most first salespeople spend their first three months working out who actually buys. We do that part first. For four weeks we do the job: contact 100 of your buyers, book the qualified ones into your diary and write the playbook. Then we recruit the person to run it.

See the founding offer 3 founding places. Stop any time with 7 days' notice.
How the four weeks run

You see progress every day and a report every Friday.

Week 1

Build and start

We agree your buyers and who to leave out, build a list of 100 named decision makers, write two versions of the message and start outreach.

Week 2

Reach all 100

LinkedIn, email and phone. First discovery calls, with qualified meetings booked into your diary.

Week 3

Follow up

A second round of follow-ups and calls for everyone not yet reached. We keep booking meetings.

Week 4

Meet and hand over

Meetings booked earlier take place. We write the playbook and the hiring recommendation, then hand everything over.

We open and qualifyFinding, contacting and qualifying buyers, in your name, with messages you approve.
You meet and closeYou run the sales meetings and close your own deals. About 3 hours a week of your time, plus the calls.
After week 4

Hire, in 4 to 8 weeks

We recruit against the playbook, test shortlisted candidates on a mock call using your real pitch, and present a shortlist.

Run, if you want it

Outreach keeps going while your new hire works their notice, so the pipeline doesn't go cold.

A 12-week guarantee

If your hire leaves within 12 weeks, we find a replacement at no extra fee.

What you keep

Six things at the end of week 4, whatever you decide next.

  • Target list

    100 named decision makers, each with their status and the last thing they said.

  • Live pipeline

    Every conversation opened, with notes and an agreed next step.

  • Message results

    Two versions tested, which one won and why.

  • Buyer evidence

    Objections heard, answers that worked, what triggers a purchase and how long buyers take.

  • Sales playbook

    Who to sell to, what to say and realistic weekly targets for a salesperson.

  • Hiring recommendation

    Hire now, wait, or don't hire for outbound. If hire: the scorecard, salary range, advert and a mock-call test.

If outbound doesn't work for you, you learn that for the price of the pilot, not a year's salary.

Is this for you

It works best when the role can be tested in four weeks.

A good fit

  • Funded B2B company, seed to Series A
  • The founder has closed some deals personally
  • Deals close in weeks or a few months
  • A first sales hire planned in the next 6 months

Not a fit

  • Pre-revenue, with no customers yet
  • Sales cycles of 6 months or more with procurement. We'll say so and point you elsewhere
  • Self-serve products sold without a salesperson
  • Selling to consumers
