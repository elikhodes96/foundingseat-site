How it works

You can stop after any step. Most founders do the first and third: the four weeks' fee comes off the hiring fee.

  1. 1

    Prove

    Four weeks

    We agree who to target, build your first 100 prospects and check each one by hand, test two messages, and book qualified meetings. You finish with a playbook and an honest hiring recommendation.

  2. 2

    Run

    Six-month term, if you want it

    We keep going as your sales team: 100 new prospects a month, outreach by LinkedIn, email and phone, your CRM kept up to date, about 5 meetings a month. Teams whose sales already work can start here, with 400 prospects and a target of 20 meetings a month.

  3. 3

    Hire

    Four to eight weeks a role

    The person who ran your outreach runs the search. Shortlisted candidates do a mock call on your real pitch. If your hire leaves within 12 weeks, we replace them free.

Common questions

What's the catch with the free 10 prospects?

None. It shows you how we choose and check prospects. If you like them, we'll talk about the other 90. If not, keep them anyway.

Why not just hire a salesperson now?

Because a new person on an untested process changes two things at once. If it doesn't work, you can't tell whether it was the person or the process. The four weeks tests the process first, so your hire starts on something that already works.

We already use a recruiter.

Keep them if you like. Four weeks of evidence gives any recruiter a far better brief, and your hire starts with live pipeline.

We need someone now.

The four weeks can run alongside your search. Whoever you hire inherits the pipeline.

Isn't it too early for sales?

That's what the four weeks tests, for £3,500 instead of a salary.

You don't know our sector.

Most candidates won't either. The four weeks is where we learn it, and where we find out what your hire needs to know.

What if it doesn't work?

Stop with 7 days' notice and keep everything produced. If outbound doesn't work for you, you've found out before committing a salary.

We already have a sales team. Is this for us?

Yes, if your closers need more meetings. Start on Run for sales teams: £1,500 a month plus £250 per qualified meeting. We build 400 new prospects a month and call them using your script, book about 10 qualified meetings in month one and aim for 20 a month after that, straight into their diaries. We don't rebuild your sales process; it already works.

We already pay an outbound agency.

Most charge £2,000 to £8,000 a month whether meetings turn up or not. Part of our fee is only paid when a meeting passes all six checks, and every prospect is checked by hand. Compare the last three months with what we'd commit to.

Who builds the list?

We do, and we check every contact by hand. On Run we build 100 new prospects every month, or work from your own list for £500 a month less.

How often do you contact each prospect?

Up to five times over about three weeks: a personal first message, a short follow-up, something useful, an email or call, and a final note. We stop the moment someone replies or asks us to, and never send more than two messages a week.

Who sends the messages?

Eli and the Founding Seat team, in your company's name, using messages you approve. We don't buy contact lists or automate LinkedIn.
